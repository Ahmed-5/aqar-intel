# Coast Towers – Jeddah

**Developer:** Al-Ufuq Real Estate Development Co.
**Location:** Ash Shati, Jeddah
**Launch:** May 2024
**Expected handover:** June 2027
**Total units:** 420

## About the project
Twin residential towers on the Jeddah corniche with direct sea views.

## Unit types and sizes
- **Apartment**: 205 units, 95–210 sqm, prices from SAR 963,000 to SAR 2,770,000.
- **Duplex**: 215 units, 240–360 sqm, prices from SAR 2,743,000 to SAR 5,065,000.

## Amenities
- Infinity pool
- Gym
- 24/7 security
- Smart parking
- Sea terrace

## Finishing specifications
- Premium finishes: porcelain flooring, marble entrance lobbies.
- Fitted kitchens with quartz worktops.
- Concealed split air-conditioning in all rooms.
- Basic smart-home package (lighting and smart lock).
- 10-year structural warranty and 1-year finishing warranty.

## Location
The project is located in Ash Shati, Jeddah, with quick access to main roads and services.
