# Yasmin Suburb – Riyadh

**Developer:** Al-Ufuq Real Estate Development Co.
**Location:** Al Yasmin, Riyadh
**Launch:** September 2024
**Expected handover:** March 2027
**Total units:** 520

## About the project
An integrated suburb targeting young families under housing-support programmes.

## Unit types and sizes
- **Apartment**: 271 units, 95–210 sqm, prices from SAR 571,000 to SAR 1,457,000.
- **Townhouse**: 249 units, 220–320 sqm, prices from SAR 1,360,000 to SAR 2,325,000.

## Amenities
- Retail centre
- International school
- Gardens
- Ladies' club
- Grand mosque

## Finishing specifications
- Premium finishes: porcelain flooring, marble entrance lobbies.
- Fitted kitchens with quartz worktops.
- Concealed split air-conditioning in all rooms.
- Basic smart-home package (lighting and smart lock).
- 10-year structural warranty and 1-year finishing warranty.

## Location
The project is located in Al Yasmin, Riyadh, with quick access to main roads and services.
