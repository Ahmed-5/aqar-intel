# Al Rawabi District – Dammam

**Developer:** Al-Ufuq Real Estate Development Co.
**Location:** Al Rawabi, Dammam
**Launch:** September 2023
**Expected handover:** June 2026
**Total units:** 260

## About the project
Spacious family villas minutes from King Fahd Road.

## Unit types and sizes
- **Duplex**: 124 units, 240–360 sqm, prices from SAR 1,233,000 to SAR 2,149,000.
- **Villa**: 136 units, 320–520 sqm, prices from SAR 1,719,000 to SAR 3,018,000.

## Amenities
- Mosque
- Playgrounds
- Central market
- Cycling lanes

## Finishing specifications
- Premium finishes: porcelain flooring, marble entrance lobbies.
- Fitted kitchens with quartz worktops.
- Concealed split air-conditioning in all rooms.
- Basic smart-home package (lighting and smart lock).
- 10-year structural warranty and 1-year finishing warranty.

## Location
The project is located in Al Rawabi, Dammam, with quick access to main roads and services.
