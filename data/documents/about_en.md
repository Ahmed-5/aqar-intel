# About Al-Ufuq Real Estate Development Co.

Al-Ufuq Real Estate Development Co. (CR 1010987654) is a Saudi developer of integrated residential communities in Riyadh, Jeddah, the Eastern Province and Madinah. We work with the Ministry of Housing and the Sakani programme to deliver quality homes with flexible payment plans.
